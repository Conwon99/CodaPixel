# Package Recommendation Guide

## Quick Decision Framework

### Ask These Questions:
1. **What's your main goal?** (Get online / Get found / Grow fast)
2. **How many customers do you need?** (Just a few / Steady stream / Rapid growth)
3. **Do you want to manage marketing yourself?** (Yes / No / Some help)
4. **What's your budget comfort level?** (£39 / £79 / £119+)

---

## **Starter Package (£39/month)**

### Who It's For:
- **New businesses** just getting started online
- **Established businesses** with a website that just needs maintenance
- **Businesses on a tight budget** who need the essentials
- **Businesses that handle their own marketing** and just need a reliable website
- **Service businesses** with steady referral traffic (don't need to attract new customers online)

### How to Describe It:
*"The Starter package is perfect if you already have a website or you're just getting started and need the basics covered. You get hosting, security updates, and your site indexed on Google - everything to keep your website running smoothly. It's ideal if you're handling your own marketing or you get most of your customers through word-of-mouth."*

### Key Indicators:
✅ "I just need a website that works"  
✅ "I get most customers through referrals"  
✅ "I handle my own social media and marketing"  
✅ "I'm on a tight budget right now"  
✅ "My website just needs to be maintained"

### What They Get:
- A reliable online presence
- No technical headaches (hosting & maintenance handled)
- Basic Google visibility
- Security and backups covered
